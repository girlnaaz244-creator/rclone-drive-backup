Privacy Policy

This application is used by its owner with rclone for personal Google Drive backups.

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive to list and download files to a local computer.

Google Drive content is downloaded to a local folder controlled by the owner. This website does not collect, sell, or share Google Drive content.

OAuth credentials are managed by rclone and stored in the owner's local configuration.

The owner can revoke Google account access at any time.
